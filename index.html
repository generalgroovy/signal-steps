How the rules work

Both needs two on signals. Either needs at least one. One only needs exactly one. Opposite flips its first signal.

Rules pass their answer down the board. A later rule can use that answer, so a few simple rules can express a bigger idea. This is a local logic playground; it never controls your computer.
